Crash.App

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 15, 2026
The short version All incident data you enter — including photos, injury details, and location — stays on your device. Crash App does not transmit your personal information to any server, does not create accounts, and does not track you.

1. Who We Are

Crash App is a mobile application that helps users document vehicle accidents at the scene and generate incident reports. The app is operated as an independent product. For privacy questions, contact us at harley@kyber-llc.com.

2. Data We Collect

Crash App is designed to collect only what you explicitly enter during report creation. This includes:

  • Incident details you type: your name, incident type, date and time, location description, and notes
  • Injury and vehicle damage selections you make within the app
  • Pain level and symptom details you enter
  • Photos you choose to add from your device's camera or photo library

We do not collect any data in the background, in the absence of your direct input, or without your knowledge.

3. How Your Data Is Stored

All report data — including incident details and photos — is stored exclusively in your device's local storage (localStorage). No data is transmitted to any external server, database, or cloud service operated by Crash App.

Reports remain on your device until you delete the app or clear your browser/app data. Crash App has no mechanism to access, retrieve, or delete your data remotely.

4. Geolocation

Crash App may request access to your device's location for one specific purpose: to find licensed accident attorneys near you in the Legal section of the app. This is done on demand — only when you tap "Find Attorneys Near Me."

  • Location is used only to query a third-party open mapping service (OpenStreetMap / Overpass API) for nearby attorney offices.
  • Your precise GPS coordinates are not stored permanently in the app or transmitted to Crash App servers.
  • Location access is optional. The app functions fully without it.

5. Photos

Photos you add to a report are stored locally on your device as part of the saved report data. They are never automatically uploaded, shared, or transmitted.

Photos are only shared when you explicitly tap the Share button and choose a sharing method (email, SMS, or your device's native share sheet). The act of sharing is entirely your choice and is performed through your device's own apps — Crash App does not handle the transmission itself.

6. Third-Party Services

Crash App makes limited use of third-party services:

  • Nominatim (OpenStreetMap): Used to reverse-geocode your coordinates into a city/state label for display. No personally identifying information is sent — only latitude and longitude coordinates.
  • Overpass API (OpenStreetMap): Used to query for attorney office locations near your GPS coordinates. Only coordinates are sent; no personal data.
  • Google Fonts: The app loads fonts (DM Sans, DM Serif Display) from Google Fonts CDN. This involves a standard HTTPS request to Google's servers, subject to Google's own privacy policy.
  • Google AdSense (if enabled): The app may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. If active, Google may use cookies and device identifiers to serve relevant ads in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalized ads at adssettings.google.com.

7. Analytics and Tracking

Crash App does not use any analytics platform (such as Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Firebase Analytics, or similar services). We do not track how you use the app, which screens you visit, or how long you spend in the app.

8. Accounts and Authentication

Crash App does not require account creation, registration, or login of any kind. No username, email address, or password is collected by the app itself.

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with any third party for marketing purposes or any other commercial reason. The only data that ever leaves your device is what you explicitly send using the Share function.

10. Children's Privacy

Crash App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to the app, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

11. Your Rights and Data Deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you are in full control:

  • To delete a report: use the app's delete functionality.
  • To delete all app data: clear the app's storage in your device settings, or uninstall the app.
  • We have no copies of your data and cannot delete it remotely on your behalf.

12. Security

Report data is stored in your device's local storage, which is sandboxed per app by your operating system. Crash App does not transmit your saved report data to Kyber LLC servers. Optional attorney-search features may send approximate location coordinates to third-party mapping providers only when you request that feature. Keep your device passcode enabled for an additional layer of protection.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the Effective Date at the top of this page. Continued use of Crash App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
