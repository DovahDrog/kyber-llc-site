Help build a new website or update an existing website, with agreed ongoing changes within agreed scope. Recurring authorized website bug/vulnerability checks focus on the site and access you have explicit authority to review.

Website work defined together

  • Build or update: agree on pages, features, content responsibilities, and the change backlog.
  • Recurring checks: authorized bug and vulnerability review with approved methods, test accounts, and data.
  • Follow-through: findings, priorities, and agreed fix verification, with limitations recorded.

The monthly plan is not unlimited website work, not a full penetration test, and not a 24/7 SOC. There is no security guarantee. New build, onboarding, and major changes are defined before agreement; no fixed setup charge is declared.

Assessment or penetration test?

A vulnerability assessment identifies and prioritizes weaknesses. A penetration test adds controlled manual validation of selected attack paths and impact, where explicitly authorized. The proposal states which you are buying, the testing depth, and what will not be tested.

Raw scanner output is not a full penetration test. Tool findings need review, validation where permitted, contextual analysis, and a documented account of limitations. We do not represent an untested path as a confirmed exploit.
Physical and combined assessments are proposed services, not available for scheduling. We are accepting non-binding scope inquiries only. Field work and full adversary simulation remain unavailable until activity-specific legal/licensing review, demonstrated competence, insurance, written authorization, and capability review are complete. An inquiry is not a booking or authorization to test.

The proposed plan includes two office visits per calendar month—for example, the first and third weeks by agreement. Review the office’s everyday configuration and observable security conditions without invasive physical testing.

Scope areas to discuss—not a commitment to delivery

  • Wi-Fi/security configuration help and an authorized low-impact vulnerability review within agreed scope.
  • A non-invasive observational office-security walkthrough: agreed entry points, visitor processes, and equipment placement.
  • Findings, practical priorities, and agreed fix verification; no covert entry, bypass attempts, or disruption in monthly care.

Permission and safety are prerequisites

Written authorization must come from the appropriate asset or site authority. Tenant, landlord, provider, or other third-party permissions must be resolved where needed. The scope names allowed actions, prohibited actions, emergency contacts, stop conditions, and escalation procedures.

Subject to scope and capability review. Physical testing is not currently offered or available for scheduling. Covert entry, social engineering, destructive testing, and disruption are not included by default. No activity starts until the specific methods and safety requirements are agreed.

Kyber Website & Office Care would combine agreed website work and recurring authorized checks with two office visits per calendar month. Findings, priorities, and agreed fix verification connect the work rather than leave separate lists of problems.

One scoped monthly plan

  • A shared objective, written authorization, and rules of engagement covering both environments.
  • Agreed website changes, visit windows, and low-impact review methods.
  • A consolidated findings record that distinguishes confirmed issues, observations, and untested assumptions.

Coordinated scope, not a blanket promise

Combined engagements and full adversary simulation are not available for scheduling until all readiness gates are met. A combined assessment does not automatically include a full red-team operation, covert activity, or every available technique.

The planned price is $1,600/month within agreed scope. The package remains proposed, not available for scheduling. Any separately quoted work identifies scoping, reporting, travel, and retest requirements. Qualifying referrals have separate recurring-service discount terms.

Our engagement process →
Scope and limitations
Assets and locations covered, testing dates, methods used, exclusions, and areas that could not be verified.
Executive summary
Decision-ready context for leadership, business impact, and priorities.
Technical findings and evidence
Affected assets, validation details, risk rationale, and evidence with sensitive material minimized. Unconfirmed observations are labeled separately.
Remediation guidance and debrief
Practical recommendations and a walkthrough for your team. Implementation assistance is separate unless included in the scope.
Agreed fix verification / Retest
Monthly care includes agreed fix verification within scope. The proposal defines the fixes to verify, window, access requirements, and any separate retest fee for expanded work. The retest record identifies resolved, unresolved, and not-verified items; new scope requires a new agreement.
