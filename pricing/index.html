Common questions

Before you decide.

Does $600/month cover a new website?

Yes. Website Care includes one initial website design with page count agreed beforehand, for a new business marketing website or a refresh of an existing website. It is included once when you join. Then up to 10 small update requests per month, plus one new page or substantial section per month in addition to those updates; size and complexity are agreed beforehand and requests are handled one at a time while subscribed. You or your web professional install and publish. Scope and timing are agreed before work begins.

Can I keep requesting changes?

Yes. The plan includes up to 10 small update requests per month, plus one new page or substantial section per month in addition to those updates; size and complexity are agreed beforehand and requests are handled one at a time while subscribed. Small updates include replacing supplied text or images, changing colors, or adjusting an existing section. Work beyond this monthly scope, full website redesigns, and advanced functionality such as booking systems, ecommerce, and custom integrations are separately scoped and quoted. We confirm timing, share a preview, and deliver approved templates and files for you or your web professional to install and publish.

Does each month include a full website redesign?

No. The subscription includes one initial website design when you join, with page count agreed beforehand. Each month then includes up to 10 small update requests, plus one new page or substantial section in addition to those updates. Size and complexity are agreed beforehand; requests are handled one at a time. A full website redesign is separately scoped and quoted.

Is the new page or section part of the 10 small updates?

No. One new page or substantial section per month is included in addition to the up to 10 small update requests per month. We agree on its size and complexity beforehand. Requests are handled one at a time. Booking systems, ecommerce, custom integrations, and other advanced functionality are separately scoped and quoted.

Is there a setup fee or a long-term commitment?

Website Care is $600/month, month-to-month, with no setup fee. Scope, timing, and subscription terms are agreed before work begins.

How does cancellation work?

Cancel before renewal; cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid period, and service continues through that period.

How do I start Website Care?

Email Harley with your business name, current website if you have one, and your priorities. There is no public checkout; an inquiry creates no subscription. We agree on the work and subscription terms first.

For separate security work, is a vulnerability scan the same as a penetration test?

No. A scan can inform an assessment. A penetration test requires scoped manual validation and analysis of the evidence, not just raw scanner output. The proposal identifies the depth you are buying.

Is retesting part of the combined preview?

Agreed fix verification is part of the proposed monthly scope. The retest scope, window, access requirements, and any separate fee for expanded work are defined upfront. New assets or expanded testing require a scope change.

How would payment for the combined plan work?

Website & Office Care is a planned combined service, not an open checkout. There is no public checkout. Payment and service terms must be agreed after readiness review; a scope inquiry creates no subscription.

The client pays Kyber directly; insurance reimbursement is not required. This is separate from Kyber’s own professional coverage, which remains a readiness requirement—not a claim that coverage is in place.

What if we are looking for CodeFlow?

CodeFlow is a separate existing product. Its product resources, proposals, and agreements remain separate from Website Care, Website & Office Care, and security-testing services.
