Referral terms · both customers earn 15% off

When service becomes available, both the referrer and the referred customer earn 15% off recurring monthly service after the referred customer’s first payment clears. The discount begins on the next billing cycle. Identify the referrer at signup.

At the current $1,600/month base, each qualifying customer pays $1,360/month—a saving of $240/month each. Maximum 15% per account; the discount does not stack, including for additional referrals.

Each discount lasts for each customer’s own uninterrupted subscription, independently of the other customer. If one customer cancels, that does not end the other customer’s discount. Cancelling and restarting ends that customer’s discount; restarting does not restore it.

The discount applies to recurring monthly service only, not separately quoted projects or hardware. The percentage applies to the recurring rate; the example is not a fixed dollar price promise.

Referral rewards are not payment for reviews; disclose the benefit when recommending us publicly.

Common questions

Before you discuss fit.

Is a vulnerability scan the same as a penetration test?

No. A scan can inform an assessment. A penetration test requires scoped manual validation and analysis of the evidence, not just raw scanner output. The proposal identifies the depth you are buying.

Is retesting included?

Agreed fix verification is part of the proposed monthly scope. The retest scope, window, access requirements, and any separate fee for expanded work are defined upfront. New assets or expanded testing require a scope change.

How would payment work?

This is a planned monthly service, not an app subscription or an open checkout. There is no public checkout. Payment and service terms must be agreed after readiness review; a scope inquiry creates no subscription.

The client pays Kyber directly; insurance reimbursement is not required. This is separate from Kyber’s own professional coverage, which remains a readiness requirement—not a claim that coverage is in place.

What if we are looking for CodeFlow?

CodeFlow is a separate existing product. Its product resources, proposals, and agreements remain separate from Website & Office Care and security-testing services.

Physical and combined assessments are proposed services, not available for scheduling. We are accepting non-binding scope inquiries only. Field work and full adversary simulation remain unavailable until activity-specific legal/licensing review, demonstrated competence, insurance, written authorization, and capability review are complete. An inquiry is not a booking or authorization to test.
