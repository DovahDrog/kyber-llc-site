CodeFlow boundary

CodeFlow is AI-assisted workflow support. It organizes information, surfaces possible issue categories, flags uncertainty, and drafts review-ready case packet language for authorized human review. It does not make official decisions, issue official documents, impose penalties, or replace staff judgment.

The public browser demo is for sanitized sample data only. Do not upload sensitive active-case records, private resident information, or confidential documents into the public demo.
