Sales rule

Sell systems, not generic AI.

Every offer should be named, outcome-specific, priced or proposal-gated, and tied to a proof asset or demo. Kyber should not sound like a vague AI agency.

Compliance rule

Regulated workflows stay gated.

Law, municipal enforcement, healthcare/HIPAA, SMS, AI voice, financial, and payment workflows require the relevant approval, consent, review, or vendor gate before activation.
