What do you need to understand about your systems or spaces? Send Harley a short, non-sensitive outline. We can discuss fit, boundaries, timing, and a scoped quote before any commitment to testing.

A useful first email
can be simple.

  1. Your organization and role.
    Who owns or is responsible for the systems or facilities?
  2. The type of assessment.
    Digital, physical, combined, or help deciding.
  3. A high-level scope.
    Approximate asset or application count, number of sites, and general location. No credentials or detailed target lists yet.
  4. The question and timing.
    What prompted the review, when you need it, and any known operational constraints.

An inquiry is not authorization to test. We confirm authority, scope, and capability fit before any engagement is accepted. Physical/combined inquiries are non-binding planning discussions, not bookings.
