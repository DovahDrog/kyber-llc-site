Staff-final-review-only

AI suggests and assembles. City staff decides.

CodeFlow is not autonomous enforcement, legal advice, or a replacement for the municipal system of record. The authority model is staff-final-review-only: authorized city staff verifies, edits, approves, and enters or attaches final material into the official system.

  • Possible violations are suggestions for human review.
  • Notice preparation requires property-appraiser/parcel verification.
  • Public-records exports stay subject to municipal policy and authorized review.
