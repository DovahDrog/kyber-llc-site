1. Capture

Field notes, photos, issue type, location, parcel context, and staff observations are organized into a structured draft.

2. Intervene

The system flags missing official-source support, permit dependencies, existing-case collision risk, and review gaps.

3. Review

Authorized staff review the packet, edit notes, decide next steps, and preserve an audit trail before official process.

Integration posture

Export-first until the city-specific path is verified.

CodeFlow should not be sold as a universal Tyler/EnerGov, MGO, Accela, OpenGov, Citizenserve, eTRAKiT, Click2Gov, GIS, or property-record replacement. The City Pack records the incumbent stack, login-required surfaces, public lookup paths, and the verified export or attachment workflow.

Procurement trust

Designed for municipal review realities.

  • Data boundary: setup scope defines what sources, records, and staff users are included.
  • Public-records posture: drafts and logs are treated as workflow records to be governed by the municipality’s policies.
  • Human approval: staff retain review ownership and official process authority.
  • Setup timeline: start with one department, one City Pack, limited issue families, and measurable review outcomes.
