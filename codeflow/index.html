Pilot system

  • City Pack built from official municipal sources.
  • Case queue with source-gap and dependency flags.
  • Review-ready packet drafts for staff approval.
  • Subscription/paywall access for provisioned users.

1. Capture

Field notes, photos, issue type, location, parcel context, and staff observations are organized into a structured draft.

2. Intervene

The system flags missing official-source support, permit dependencies, existing-case collision risk, and review gaps.

3. Review

Authorized staff review the packet, edit notes, decide next steps, and preserve an audit trail before official process.

Procurement trust

Designed for municipal review realities.

  • Data boundary: pilot scope defines what sources, records, and staff users are included.
  • Public-records posture: drafts and logs are treated as workflow records to be governed by the municipality’s policies.
  • Human approval: staff retain review ownership and official process authority.
  • Pilot timeline: start with one department, one city pack, limited issue families, and measurable review outcomes.

What a pilot includes
