Safety model

AI drafts. Staff decides.

CodeFlow is designed to reduce clerical friction and source gaps while keeping review authority with department staff. It supports a safer workflow by keeping final decisions, edits, and official action with authorized staff.

Boundary: CodeFlow supports authorized human review only; department staff controls any official next step.
