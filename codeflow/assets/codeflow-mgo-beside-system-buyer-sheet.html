Kyber CodeFlow buyer sheet

If you already use MGO, why CodeFlow?

CodeFlow does not replace MGO/MyGovernmentOnline or any incumbent municipal system of record. CodeFlow sits beside it as the workflow-discipline layer that helps staff separate sources, catch gaps or conflicts, and prepare cleaner review-ready packets before official action.

What CodeFlow is

  • City-specific workflow support for municipal code review teams.
  • Source separation for field photos, PAPA/property appraiser, GIS, permits, code references, records notes, and staff activity.
  • Review gates before official action.
  • Packet prep for staff to verify, export, attach, or enter into the official system.

What CodeFlow is not

  • Not a replacement for MGO, Tyler/EnerGov, Accela, OpenGov, Citizenserve, eTRAKiT, Click2Gov, GIS, or any official system of record.
  • Not autonomous enforcement.
  • Not legal advice or final violation authority.
  • Not fake “integrates with everything” software.
  • Not automatic notices, citations, fines, or liens.

The plain answer

MGO can remain the official system. CodeFlow improves the messy source-to-packet work around it.

If MGO already fully solves source conflicts, parcel/owner verification, packet prep, records-readiness, and staff review gates in daily workflow, CodeFlow may not be necessary. But if staff still assembles and verifies the messy source trail around each case, CodeFlow is built for that gap.
