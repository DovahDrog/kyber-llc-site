Buyer path

The clean sales model

1. Questionnaire

The city or department fills out a scope questionnaire so Kyber can identify source complexity, departments, staff roles, case volume, and procurement constraints.

2. Source review

The buyer provides official code links, ordinance PDFs, GIS/parcel/property sources, workflows, forms, records paths, and templates.

3. Exact quote

Kyber confirms the setup, monthly subscription, optional training/travel, timeline, inclusions, exclusions, and contract/payment path.

4. Build + launch

After SOW/payment approval, Kyber builds the private account, City Pack, staff roles, review gates, packet templates, and QA path.

Positioning correction: Do not lead with a separate paid pilot. Default to setup + monthly. If an evaluation is required, frame it as scoped setup / limited first-month use credited toward production setup.

What setup covers

  • Private city/department account provisioning.
  • City Pack buildout from official codes, ordinances, maps, parcel/property, and workflows.
  • Staff accounts, roles, permissions, and review gates.
  • Review-ready packet template configuration.
  • Manual export process or scoped integration review.
  • Municipal system-stack mapping.
  • Launch QA, onboarding, and handoff.
  • Initial staff-final-review workflow configuration.

What monthly covers

Access, hosting, usage, support

Continued use of the private CodeFlow account, hosted backend, user access, case/packet workflow, AI processing, storage, admin support, workflow QA, and issue resolution.

City Pack maintenance

Ongoing updates to official source mappings, workflows, templates, staff-review configuration, source confidence, and export/readiness posture as the account evolves.

Guardrails

What this is — and what it is not

Say this

  • CodeFlow prepares review-ready municipal workflow intelligence.
  • City staff retains final review and official process authority.
  • The City Pack adapts to the municipality’s official sources, workflow, staff roles, and export path.
  • The monthly fee covers maintained sources, workflow QA, support, and Operations+ review.

Do not say this

  • The AI enforces code.
  • CodeFlow replaces the city’s system of record.
  • CodeFlow automatically sends official notices.
  • CodeFlow gives legal advice.
  • CodeFlow integrates with every incumbent system without city-specific verification.
Contract/payment note: Stripe Checkout or invoice is only the payment rail. Municipal sale should be paired with SOW/MSA/PO or city-approved services agreement before production activation.

Recommended Palm Beach Gardens close line

“For Palm Beach Gardens, we would frame this as a mid-size / multi-department Operations+ deployment: $20,000 setup, $10,000/mo baseline, and $12,000/mo if the city wants the full managed Operations+ layer with quarterly source/workflow reviews.”
