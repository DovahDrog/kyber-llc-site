System-of-record boundary

Already using MGO or another municipal platform?

CodeFlow does not replace MGO, Tyler/EnerGov, Accela, OpenGov, GIS, or any official system of record. It sits beside those systems as a workflow-discipline layer for source separation, conflict handling, review gates, records readiness, and review-ready packet prep.
