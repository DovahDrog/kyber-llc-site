What to say

“The city does not adapt to CodeFlow. CodeFlow adapts to the city.”

Each municipality gets a private City Pack and account shaped around its official sources, code sections, staff roles, review process, and export path.

That is the premium difference between a generic portal and a managed municipal workflow system.

Authority boundary

staff-final-review-only. CodeFlow suggests, assembles, flags, drafts, and exports review packets. Authorized city staff verifies, edits, approves, and enters or attaches final material in the official city system.

  • No autonomous enforcement.
  • No legal advice.
  • No false universal integration claims.
  • No automatic official notices.

Buyer class

Palm Beach Gardens should be framed as a mid-size / multi-department Operations+ deployment.

A full Operations+ deployment — not a narrow placeholder — unless they force a limited first scope. The serious ask is $20,000 setup + $10,000/mo baseline, with $12,000/mo defensible for Operations+.

System-of-record boundary

Already using MGO or another municipal platform?

CodeFlow does not replace MGO, Tyler/EnerGov, Accela, OpenGov, GIS, or any official system of record. It sits beside those systems as a workflow-discipline layer for source separation, conflict handling, review gates, records readiness, and review-ready packet prep.
