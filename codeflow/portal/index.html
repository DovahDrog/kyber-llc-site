Access doctrine

Organization license. Staff accounts. No shared password.

Municipal workflows need auditability. CodeFlow should not use one password passed around a department. Each paying organization gets an account, and authorized employees receive staff logins so drafts, edits, approvals, and exports can be traced.

  • Subscription current: organization active
  • Subscription lapsed: access paused
  • Individual staff users under the organization
  • Role-based access for reviewer/admin functions
  • Audit log for generated packets and human edits

Safety boundary

CodeFlow does not issue enforcement actions.

The system supports research, possible issue classification, documentation, and draft packet preparation. Authorized municipal staff remain responsible for facts, code citations, cure periods, responsible parties, approval, posting, mailing, and any official action.

Internal IP boundary

Proprietary setup materials stay private.

Kyber's city-source scaffolds and internal operating methods are not sold or exposed as downloads. Customers get configured workflows, source-backed outputs, checklists, and support — not internal prompts, sensitive active-case data, or unreconciled city-specific records.
