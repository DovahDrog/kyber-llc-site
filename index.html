Sources, calculations & what these numbers mean

[30] Cyber attacks: Check Point Research’s July 2026 report gives 2,336 weekly cyber attacks per organization in its observed dataset. Dividing by seven gives 333.71, rounded to approximately 334 per day. This is a weekly-average conversion, not a daily count or a representative forecast for every business. The article does not state the sample size or a complete event-counting methodology.

[41] Phishing: APWG’s Q2 2026 report, pages 2–3 (PDF), published August 28, 2026, records 425,808 unique reported phishing sites in June. Dividing by June’s 30 days gives 14,193.6, rounded to approximately 14,194 per day. APWG calls these sites “attacks”; one site can support many URLs or messages. Reports come from member companies, research partners, and the public—not a census of all phishing, all social engineering, or successful victims.

The figures cover different populations and periods, may overlap, and are not added together. They are external industry context, not Kyber customer results or a guarantee that testing prevents every incident. Source review: September 7, 2026.

The Website Care process

Changes you request.
Publication you approve.

Agree on one website, the initial build or refresh, scope, timing, and subscription terms before work begins. Every change starts with a clear request—not assumed permission.

  1. 01

    Request

    Send the pages, text, photos, service details, or layout changes you need. We confirm the work and timing with you.

  2. 02

    Preview

    We design and develop the agreed changes, then share a preview for your review.

  3. 03

    Approval

    Review the content and layout. We resolve agreed changes and get your approval before publication.

  4. 04

    Publish

    With client permission and the required access, we publish the reviewed work and check the affected pages.

Security services follow a separate scope.

Digital assessments require written authorization, rules of engagement, third-party permissions, emergency contacts, stop conditions, and capability review. Testing depth, evidence handling, remediation support, and any retest are agreed separately. Website Care is not authorization to test.
