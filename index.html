SMS consent

Text messaging is used for requested business communication only.

Kyber LLC sends SMS only when a prospect, client, or business contact provides a phone number and asks to receive text communication, submits an intake or booking request, or otherwise gives consent during a direct conversation with Kyber.

Opt-in language

When a form, calendar, or direct intake flow asks for SMS permission, the consent language identifies Kyber LLC and the expected message purpose.

I consent to receive non-marketing text messages from Kyber LLC about appointment scheduling, follow-up to my request, and service coordination. Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

Message types

  • Appointment confirmations and reminders.
  • Follow-up after a contact requests information.
  • Missed-call or text-back responses.
  • Service coordination and booking support.

Opt-out support

Recipients can reply STOP to unsubscribe from SMS messages or HELP for assistance. Consent to receive SMS is not a condition of purchasing services from Kyber LLC.

Kyber does not sell or share SMS opt-in data or phone numbers with third parties for their marketing.
