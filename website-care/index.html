Before you decide

Website Care, explained.

Is this just website maintenance?

No. Kyber Website Care includes ongoing website design and development. The agreed scope can begin with a new website or a refresh of an existing site, followed by requested content, layout, and page changes while subscribed.

What do we agree on before starting?

One business marketing website, the initial build or refresh, requested changes, content responsibilities, access, timing, and subscription terms. We confirm those details before work begins, rather than assume every site or request is the same.

Can I request changes throughout the subscription?

Yes. Send your text, photo, service, layout, or page requests. We review each request against the agreed scope and confirm timing. A complex application, ecommerce project, integration, or expanded scope needs a separate discussion.

Does this include office visits or security testing?

No. The website-only plan excludes office visits, Wi-Fi work, physical-security services, and penetration testing. It does not promise leads, search rankings, or security outcomes. The separate planned Website & Office Care package is described below.

How do I start?

Email Harley with your business name, current website if you have one, and the pages or changes you need. There is no public checkout. An inquiry creates no subscription; scope, timing, and subscription terms are agreed before work begins.
